Every part of the kit, printed as a blueprint.

The same components as the reference page, re-seeded with seven colours: Prussian-blue panels, pale linework, drafting yellow for the one live thing and red pencil for limits and faults. Nothing in the kit is overridden.

The labels come from a tunnel drive, so each part reads as it would on a real job. Every control works.
