# Lab: blueprint tunnel drive

Scope: lab/blueprint.html, a lab example that re-seeds Calibrated as a cyanotype blueprint. Mode: Operate.
Audience: the tunnel engineer on the night shift of a sewer drive. Task: read where the boring machine is, what ground is ahead, and set the face pressure for the canal crossing before the machine reaches it.
Constraints: kit loaded live from skills/*/assets; seeds only for colour; original project, place and machine names; illustrative data labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The drive is a long section on the ruler, not a progress bar or a stat-tile dashboard. The decision is a pressure drawn between two limits.
OWN-WORLD: Calibrated panels in Prussian blue on a deeper blue desk; pale cyan-white linework; drafting-yellow lamp for the machine and the setpoint; red-pencil coral for limits; strata as distinct hatches; faint drafting grid; no glow except the star.
STORY: The engineer sees the machine 7 rings from the canal and a shift behind, drags the face pressure inside the window, sees settlement and clear time re-measure, issues it to the shift.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead with drive and clock; headline with the clear time; full-width long section (ground, canal, strata, built vs planned, machine lit, ruler in chainage) with a title strip.
FORM: Tunnel drive progress, list position 4, seed 5cfd040f.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished
