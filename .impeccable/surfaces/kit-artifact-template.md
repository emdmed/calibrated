Scope: the kit's visual world (tokens, figure kit, components, artifact template). The demo page is a separate docs surface (see demo-index-html). Mode: operate.
Audience: developers building internal tools; people asking Claude for artifacts. Job: read a quantity at a glance; adopt the kit in their own product.

## Direction contract
THESIS: every page is an engineering drawing sheet (ISO 128): framed sheet, zone grid, title block, revision marks. Refuses the dark dev-tool dashboard and the blueprint-blue cliché.
OWN-WORLD: drafting-film white, graphite ink, two line weights plus dash-dot centre lines; a checker's highlighter yellow marks the one live thing (swipe behind ink), red markup for faults and revisions, green check for passed. Condensed technical caps (Archivo), Chivo Mono numerals, the ruled mark (a scale with one swiped graduation) as the logo. Square, flat, ruled.
STORY: the reader sees the sheet, finds the highlighted live thing, reads values off boxes, trusts the numbers.
FIRST VIEWPORT: demo opens as sheet 1: zone-framed border, title block bottom-right (name, scale, sheet, rev), headline left, the eight-mark specimen grid as the drawing.
FORM: The Drawing Sheet, pick (rank 1 of 7), seed 9c767132.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
